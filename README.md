# AI Implementation Framework: Google Cloud Platform

A strategy paper on how Google Cloud could deploy AI inside its own go-to-market and business operations. It covers which initiatives to run, how they change decision rights and roles, how to govern them, and how to roll them out in phases.

Written for MGMT 7019 at the University of Cincinnati (2026). It is a course project built from public information, framed as advice to a senior management team. It is not an internal Google document.

**Read the paper:** [`GCP_AI_Implementation_Framework.pdf`](GCP_AI_Implementation_Framework.pdf) (17 pages)

## The argument in brief

Google Cloud's main disadvantage against AWS and Azure is enterprise trust and sales execution, not technology. AI is both its strongest differentiator and its biggest credibility risk. Customers will judge Vertex AI partly by whether Google can run AI responsibly inside its own business.

## Approach

Each AI initiative is designed with a clear human role. The paper says what the model decides alone, what a person reviews, and who can override it. Every phase has measurable criteria for scaling up or rolling back.
